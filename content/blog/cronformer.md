> Try Cronformer out today [here](https://uncommonstash.com/text-to-cron).

Cronformer is a small transformer model that can translate English sentences into Cron expressions. It uses Gemma-270M as the pretrained base and has a specialized decoder head architecture to enable blazing fast non-autoregressive inference. It is trained on a synthetic dataset with more than 10M examples.
